About Nick

A practical, human approach to spoken English.

I’m a native British English speaker and TEFL-qualified English teacher. My working life has included the RAF and the NHS, followed by teaching English in schools in Thailand and Vietnam.

My teaching style is relaxed and conversational. Rather than filling a lesson with exercises, I prefer to get you speaking and then work on the areas that will make the biggest difference to your everyday English.

That might be pronunciation, intonation, natural phrasing, confidence or simply finding a better way to express something you already know how to say.

The aim is straightforward: help you feel more comfortable speaking English in the real world.
